# Exhibit illustrations

These interpretive paintings were created using the built-in ImageGen tool. They were resized and encoded as offline WebP assets. Source credit for scientific claims appears in the exhibit content. The artwork depicts contexts and reconstructions.

## origins

Asset `app/src/main/assets/exhibits/origins.webp`

```text
Scientific-educational museum exhibit landscape illustration for an Arabic Android TV science museum. A concept illustration of early Earth without plants, diffuse volcanic coastline at dawn, glassy shallow water, foreground rock with a subtle layered microbial-mat texture. Show the environment at landscape scale and suggest microbial life via the mat. No enlarged organs, no dinosaurs, no modern organisms. Use naturalistic painted textures, readable composition at 1080p, deep navy shadows, warm gold highlights and restrained teal secondary tones. Landscape 16:9 or wide 3:2 framing. No embedded labels, no text, no watermark, no border. This illustration will be visibly labelled interpretive art in the app.
```
